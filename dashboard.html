Rep Scorecard — Book Flow, Today's Actions & Guidance

"Today" = the rep's own calls & notes logged today and meetings whose start time is today (attributed by the activity's own owner, across their whole book — not just this creator). Book flow is the rep's currently-owned contacts by stage; NI/DQ shown are those still attributed to the rep (the automation strips owner on some). Guidance is rule-based on the book mix — a prompt for a 1:1, not a verdict.

Rep Coaching — 15-min 1:1 Diagnosis

Pick a rep to run a quick diagnosis across the 5 problem areas, see today's effort & stage moves, and spot-check 5 random contacts for message / process review.
Diagnosis is rule-based on live signals — a structured prompt for the 1:1, not a verdict. Re-open daily to track each rep's trend. The 5 spot-check contacts are sampled randomly from the rep's book each time; open each in HubSpot to review message quality, personalization, objection handling and whether a clear next step was set.

Rep Activity & Disposition Quality

Creator-level, on the owned ("active") book.
NI/DQ lose their owner via automation, so disposition-quality uses touch counts. "≤1 touch" = lead marked Not Interested / Disqualified after at most one logged contact.

Disposition Integrity — Audit the Kills (NI · DQ · DNP · FU-DNP)

A dead lead is a claim, not a fact. Did the rep create value before the lead went dead? Short calls on NI, or few attempts before DNP/NI, mean the disposition is suspect — a value/skill problem wearing a "dead lead" costume. If the whole cohort is red across every rep, it's the lead source, not the reps.
Audit queue — most suspect kills (fewest touches first) — open each, listen to the call & read the messages, then approve the disposition (with reason) or bounce it back to the rep's active book.
⌀call = average connected-call length on that rep's contacts in the stage — a real conversation runs minutes, not seconds. ⌀touch = average logged contacts (num_contacted_notes) before the lead was marked dead. Verdict = worst-of the NI-call-quality, NI-effort and DNP-effort signals. DQ calls can legitimately be short (spam / wrong number), so DQ is shown for context but weighted lightly. Thresholds: ⌀call good ≥120s / amber ≥45s; ⌀touch good ≥3 / amber ≥2; true DNP should have ≥3 attempts across different times.
